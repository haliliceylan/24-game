How to play

  1. Make 24. Use all the cards, each one once, with + − × ÷.
  2. Pick two cards, then an operation. The two cards merge into one dashed yellow card that shows how it was made. The first card you tap goes on the left, so 7 then 5 then − is 7 − 5. Keep merging until one card is left. When the last two cards can make 24, it happens as soon as you tap them.
  3. Or tap all the cards in the order you'd use them and skip the operations. For 7 7 5 4, tap 7, 7, 5, 4, because 7 ÷ 7 = 1, 1 + 5 = 6, 6 × 4 = 24. The first two cards you tap are combined first.
  4. Right order: the solution is shown and you get a ★. Wrong order: the cards shake, try again.
  5. You have 1 minute. After that, the two cards to start with light up in orange. 30 seconds later the solution is shown.
  6. Some hands can't make 24. When the minute runs out, a blue 5th card is added and the clock restarts. Now use all five.

Buttons

  • ↶ untap the last card, or split the last merged card
  • ⊞ change the card layout
  • » show the solution, then tap again for a new hand
  • Tap the solution to see the hand's other solutions, one by one. The cards are merged step by step, with each operation shown above them.
